A LITTLE GUIDE

Let there be light.

Choose an unlocked patakha, then tap or click the sky to launch it. Aim at the glowing star circles. Points appear when your firework bursts.

  1. Each star is worth 100 points.
  2. Hit another star within 3 seconds to grow your combo, up to 5×.
  3. You have 60 seconds. Launch before time runs out; your final fireworks will finish before your score appears.
  4. Complete the four missions in order to unlock new fireworks. Progress and unlocks are saved on this device.

Keyboard: 1–8 choose an unlocked firework. Arrow keys move your aim. Space launches. P pauses.

Free play counts toward your first mission. Star, combo, and score missions need challenge mode. Use Soft glow for gentler lighting.
