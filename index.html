LET THERE BE LIGHT

A little spark goes a long way.

  1. Choose a cracker and tap a glowing star to launch. Each hit starts at 100 points.
  2. Chain hits less than 3 seconds apart to build up to a 5× combo. A miss breaks the chain.
  3. Golden bonus stars are worth double points. Watch for them as the round progresses.
  4. Finish eight missions in order to unlock all 12 crackers. The first mission works in either mode; the others need challenge mode.

Each round lasts 60 seconds. Fireworks launched before time runs out still count. Aim for 1,000, 3,000, or 6,000 points to earn a bronze, silver, or gold round medal.

Keyboard: arrows aim; Space launches; P pauses. Keys 1–9, 0, − and = select the 12 crackers. Locked crackers must be earned first.

Sound is on by default and starts after a tap or keypress. You can mute it or change the volume. Soft glow makes the lighting gentler.
